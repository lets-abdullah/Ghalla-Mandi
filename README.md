# 🌾 Ghalla Mandi ERP

A modern ERP solution built specifically for **Ghalla Mandi businesses, grain traders, wholesalers, and commission agents (آڑھتی)**.

Ghalla Mandi ERP helps businesses manage their daily operations from a single, easy-to-use system — including sales, purchases, inventory, customer accounts, supplier accounts, payments, returns, and business reports.

---

## 📋 About

**Ghalla Mandi ERP** is designed around the real-world workflow of commodity trading businesses.

Whether you manage a grain shop, wholesale business, or commission-based trading operation, the system helps you keep your business records organized and accessible.

It brings your day-to-day business activities into one centralized platform.
